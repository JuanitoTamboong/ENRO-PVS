The primary purpose of the ENRO Permit and Extraction Volume Monitoring System is to digitize, regulate, and monitor the extraction and purchase of sand, gravel, and quarry resources by authorized permit holders. It provides management with complete operational visibility, ensures strict compliance with environmental extraction limits, and prevents illegal over-extraction.

Core Objectives and Functions

Automated Volume Deduction: Automatically deducts every recorded delivery receipt volume from the latest remaining balance to maintain an accurate, real-time ledger of allowed versus extracted materials.

Over-Extraction Prevention: Strictly blocks any transaction from being processed if the requested volume exceeds the permit holder's remaining allowed capacity.

Low-Volume Warning Alerts: Instantly triggers an on-screen warning notification when a permit holder's remaining balance drops to or below 100 cu.m.

Permit Directory & Instant Search: Allows authorized users to search permittees by name or permit number to view complete profiles, extraction rates, validity dates, and operational statuses (such as Active, Nearly Exhausted, Fully Consumed, Low Production, or Expired).

Detailed Transaction Auditing: Maintains a permanent daily record of all transactions—including dates, delivery receipt (DR) numbers, official receipt (OP) numbers, truck loads/plate numbers, previous balances, new balances, and the personnel responsible for recording the entry.

Remote Mobile & Desktop Access: Enables management and field inspectors to monitor permit holders, track daily extraction totals, and review historical logs from any location using a computer or mobile device.
